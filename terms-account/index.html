SC Compagnon — Optional web account — 11 October 2026

The service

SC Compagnon is an unofficial fan companion for tabletop play. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment or Archon Studio. Their names, rules and other protected material remain their property. The companion does not replace the official rules or physical game components.

The service is provided by Olivier Saleur, micro-entrepreneur, 15 rue des Îles, 60340 Saint-Leu-d'Esserent, France (SIRET 106 671 837 00016). Contact: support@sctmg-iacompagnon.com — +33 9 72 16 41 97.

Optional account

You can play without an account. A web account adds private storage for eligible personal armies, maps and statistics. It uses an email verification code rather than a password. Keep access to your email secure, do not share codes, and sign out on shared devices. Accounts are for your own use; do not impersonate someone else or attempt to access another person's data.

The iOS and Android apps remain offline. You can export their statistics and import the file into your web account. Files from the same source update that source's snapshot instead of adding it again. These are personal, self-reported results; they are not verified competitive rankings.

The First hour badge is awarded by the server to accounts created during the first six months after the public launch. It remains attached to that account until deletion. It has no monetary value and grants no special game advantage. Community rankings, public profiles and additional rewards are not part of this release.

Your content and sharing

Save only content you have the right to use and avoid personal information in army or map names. Do not upload rules, card images, game databases or Command Center content. The service only accepts a limited format containing your compositions and approved public catalogue references. Do not try to circumvent these limits.

Sharing is by a JSON file you choose to export. You decide whom to send it to and should check its contents first. Recipients can keep copies. Cloud storage and local device storage are separate: deleting an online record does not delete exported files or copies on other devices.

You may export your account data and delete the account from Your space. A fresh email verification is required for permanent account deletion. The privacy policy explains how personal data is processed and how to exercise your rights.

Availability and responsibilities

Keep your own exports of important data. A browser may erase local storage and online services may be temporarily unavailable. Do not use the service to distribute unlawful material, malicious code, spam or content that infringes others' rights. Reasonable technical limits apply to prevent abuse. Access may be suspended when necessary to address a security incident or unlawful use, subject to applicable law.

The companion is supplied free of charge and may contain errors. Mandatory consumer rights and liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded are unaffected. No automatic game decision should replace checking the official rule when in doubt.

Changes

Registration for the web account may be opened, paused or closed, for example during maintenance or security checks; playing without an account is unaffected. No paid service or subscription is created by the account. Material changes to these terms or the service will be disclosed before they apply.

French law applies, without depriving consumers of mandatory protection applicable in their country of habitual residence. Contact the operator first about a concern; your right to bring a matter before a competent court or authority remains unaffected.

Le service n'accepte qu'un format limité contenant vos compositions et des références approuvées du catalogue public. Ne tentez pas de contourner ces limites.

Le stockage en ligne et celui des appareils sont distincts : supprimer un élément en ligne ne supprime pas les fichiers exportés ni les copies sur d'autres appareils.

Vous pouvez exporter les données de votre compte et supprimer celui-ci depuis Ton espace. Une nouvelle vérification par email est requise pour supprimer définitivement le compte. La politique de confidentialité explique le traitement des données personnelles et l'exercice de vos droits.
