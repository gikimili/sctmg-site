Privacy Policy

SC Compagnon — Version 1.2 — 30 September 2026

In short

SC Compagnon collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no crash reporting from the app itself, no advertising, no tracking. Everything you create stays on your device.

Who is responsible

Olivier Saleur, micro-entrepreneur (sole trader), 15 rue des Îles, 60340 Saint-Leu-d'Esserent, France (SIRET 106 671 837 00016). Contact: support@sctmg-iacompagnon.com — +33 9 72 16 41 97

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user account, sends no data to us, and contains no third-party SDK that could collect data on our behalf. We do not know that you use the app, when, or how.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your data in your device's local storage (IndexedDB):

  • your games: state, army lists, dice rolls, log, scores;
  • your settings: language, display options;
  • the external data file you choose to load, if any.

This data never leaves your device unless you decide to export or share it yourself. Deleting the app (or clearing the site data in your browser) deletes this data permanently. We hold no copy and cannot recover it.

External data file

You can load a data file of your own (for example card data) into the app. It is read locally and stays on the device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Website

The sites sctmg-iacompagnon.com and .net are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical connection logs (IP address) as part of hosting the site, under its own policy. We add no cookies, no tags, no analytics.

Support and feedback by e-mail

The app can help you write a support e-mail (a rules question, a bug report, an idea, or feedback on a finished game). Nothing is ever sent automatically: the e-mail is prepared on your device and opens in your own mail app, and you choose whether to send it.

The e-mail may contain: the app version, your operating system or browser family (never the full technical string), the app's display language, and, for game feedback, an optional summary of that game (mission, army lists, bot level, score, seed, log summary) plus any player names and comment you typed yourself. No device identifier, advertising ID, or location is ever included.

Apple crash reports

If you have turned on sharing analytics with app developers in your iPhone or iPad settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), or if you test the app through TestFlight, Apple may pass crash reports and, if you choose to send it, your feedback on to us. This service is provided by Apple under its own privacy policy; the app itself sends nothing. You can turn it off at any time in the same settings.

Third-party SDKs

None. The app embeds no advertising, analytics, attribution, or crash reporting library.

Children

The app contains no restricted content, but it is not directed at children and is not designed for them. It collects nothing about anyone, of any age.

Your rights (GDPR)

Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, port, or delete on our side. You control your data directly: it is on your device, and you can delete it at any time. If you have any question about this, write to support@sctmg-iacompagnon.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

Changes

If this policy changes, the version and date at the top of this page change too, and the new version is published in the app and on the website. Material changes will be announced in the app.

Contact

Olivier Saleur — support@sctmg-iacompagnon.com
